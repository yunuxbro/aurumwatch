## Full-history FX audit status

The CBSL source series covers daily rates from 2010-05-07; the local Yahoo table has 3,082 rows (2015-01-01 to 2026-11-04). Only 2-4 Nov 2026 have been explicitly cross-checked (two material Yahoo errors on Nov 2-3 and about Rs 0.02 difference on Nov 4). Macro Lab reports its two CBSL source series agree on 1,376 of 1,381 overlapping dates, with rounding and one material discrepancy documented; that is not a Yahoo comparison. The full Yahoo-to-CBSL mismatch count is unknown, so the other 3,079 rows are not claimed verified.

The CBSL results page offers a CSV control, but direct retrieval was blocked in this workspace. The published Macro Lab compilation is CC BY 4.0 for its charts/text; rates are attributed to CBSL, and the dataset itself remains the agencies' data. Cite CBSL via Macro Lab and inspect its documented discrepancy notes when using it as a cross-check.
